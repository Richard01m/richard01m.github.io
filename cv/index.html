Experience

  • 2026 - 2026

    Guangzhou, China

    CultureLens: Steerable Human-AI Video Storytelling
    HKUST (GZ)
    Advisor: Prof. Pan Hui. An interactive video generation workbench for steering cross-cultural translation through four transcreation modes: Literal, Analogy, Explanation, and Sensation. Creator and audience studies suggest it reduces mental demand versus a general-purpose LLM baseline and reduces stereotypical perception among Western audiences.
  • 2026 - 2026

    Guangzhou, China

    RepoDirector: Repository-Grounded Generative Video
    HKUST (GZ)
    Advisor: Prof. Anyi Rao. An integrated authoring system organized around a persistent, editable storyboard for open-source promotional videos. In a within-subjects study with 18 participants, it reduced NASA-TLX workload and raised mean SUS from 41.94 to 79.58 relative to a fragmented toolchain.
  • 2026 - 2026

    Guangzhou, China

    GlyphFix: Repairing Garbled Text in AI-Generated Video
    HKUST (GZ)
    Advisor: Prof. Pan Hui. A mixed-initiative workflow for localized text repair that separates semantic content, spatial behavior, and visual appearance. In a study with 16 participants, GlyphFix required 27.2% of the mean completion time of conventional workflows.
  • 2026 - 2026

    Guangzhou, China

    Fideli3D: Geometrically Consistent 3D Data Storytelling
    HKUST (GZ)
    Advisor: Prof. Pan Hui. A multi-agent framework that transforms narrative text into geometrically consistent 3D data videos through semantic, asset, configuration, and camera-optimization layers.
  • 2026 - 2026

    Guangzhou, China

    Ink Symphony: Calligraphy to Music in VR
    HKUST (GZ)
    Advisor: Prof. Kang Zhang. A VR approach that turns Chinese calligraphy into music, drawing on the classical story of Zhi Yin. Presented as a design exploration.
  • 2024 - 2024

    Shanghai, China

    Human Preference Decoding via ERP Analysis
    Shanghai University
    Advisor: Prof. Huiran Zhang. An ERP study of preference judgments using the early N1 component and the late positive potential. Preprint: arXiv:2505.19879.
  • 2024 - 2024

    Shanghai, China

    Shanghai University
    Advisor: Prof. Liming Xin. A neural-network anti-interference controller for lake water-level balance, evaluated on a simulator of the Great Lakes system. DOI:10.1117/12.3035322.
