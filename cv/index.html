Experience

  • 2026 - 2026

    Guangzhou, China

    CultureLens: Steerable Human-AI Video Storytelling
    The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou)
    Advisor: Prof. Pan Hui. CultureLens is an interactive video generation workbench that supports cross-cultural translation through four transcreation modes: Literal, Analogy, Explanation, and Sensation, covering narrative restructuring, audiovisual presentation, and voice-over. Based on a formative study (N=8), mixed-methods studies from creator (N=18) and audience (N=54) perspectives show that it reduces mental demand versus a general-purpose LLM baseline, maintains usability, and reduces stereotypical perception among Western audiences.
  • 2026 - 2026

    Guangzhou, China

    RepoDirector: Repository-Grounded Generative Video
    The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou)
    Advisor: Prof. Anyi Rao. RepoDirector is an integrated authoring system for open-source promotional videos, centered on a persistent editable storyboard carrying scene-level narration, prompts, assets, ordering, and production status across stages. Offline validation found repository contexts generally evidence-supported but incomplete. In a within-subjects study (N=18), it reduced all NASA-TLX workload dimensions and raised SUS from 41.94 to 79.58 versus a fragmented toolchain, with scene-local interventions in 11/18 sessions, though owner judgments showed no consistent final-video quality advantage.
  • 2026 - 2026

    Guangzhou, China

    GlyphFix: Repairing Garbled Text in AI-Generated Video
    The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou)
    Advisor: Prof. Pan Hui. GlyphFix is an interactive multimodal agent that repairs garbled text in AI-generated videos via aesthetic decoupling, separating semantic restoration from generative artifacts. It employs a cascading pipeline with LLMs and VLMs for semantic inference and computer vision for spatiotemporal tracking, plus a mixed-initiative workspace that automates tracking while reserving typographic control for users.
  • 2026 - 2026

    Guangzhou, China

    Fideli3D: Geometrically Consistent 3D Data Storytelling
    The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou)
    Advisor: Prof. Pan Hui. Fideli3D is a multi-agent framework that transforms narrative text into 3D data videos via a layered pipeline. A Semantic Layer parses narrative intent into shot scripts. An Asset Layer procedurally assembles 3D entities. A Configuration Layer maps high-level intent to required visual properties. An Optimization Layer formulates camera control as a constraint satisfaction problem to derive optimal trajectories.
  • 2026 - 2026

    Guangzhou, China

    Ink Symphony: Calligraphy to Music in VR
    The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou)
    Advisor: Prof. Kang Zhang. Ink Symphony is a VR system that lets users practice calligraphy in a traditional Chinese landscape. The system captures brushstrokes, analyzes stroke speed and pressure, and generates real-time traditional Chinese music. Tempo adapts to writing speed, while music type and intensity respond to stroke weight. The final output is a synchronized calligraphy video with a context-aware melody that matches the writing process.
  • 2024 - 2024

    Human Preference Decoding via ERP Analysis
    Shanghai University
    Advisor: Prof. Huiran Zhang. Investigated neural correlates of human preference using event-related potentials, focusing on early N1 and late positive potential components. Analyzed EEG data from participants viewing a mixed-image dataset. N1 amplitude in frontal regions increased for preferred items, while LPP amplitude was larger for both preferred and non-preferred items. Proposed an integrated model combining N1 and LPP to quantify preference intensity and direction. Preprint: arXiv:2505.19879.

    Shanghai University

    Shanghai University
